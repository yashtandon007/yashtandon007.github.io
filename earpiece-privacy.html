Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

App Name: Call Volume Booster & Earpiece

Package Name: com.earspeakervolumebooster.callvolumeincreaser

Developer Name: Gold Apps Labz

Contact Email: goldappslabz@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Gold Apps Labz ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Call Volume Booster & Earpiece Android application (the "App"). The App lets you boost call and media volume, route audio to the earpiece, use the proximity sensor during calls, and adjust system volume levels.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not run our own servers. We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, call history, call audio, messages, photos, or files. The App does not record calls and does not use the microphone.

Your settings (for example boost level, theme, and which features are on) are stored only on your device and are removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall it.

2. Permissions and How They Are Used

  • Modify audio settings: to apply the volume boost and change volume levels you choose in the App.
  • Read phone state (optional): only if you turn on automatic speakerphone, so the App can tell when a call starts or ends. Phone numbers and call details are not read, stored, or shared. The App still works if you deny it.
  • Notifications and foreground service: while volume boost or earpiece features are on, the App shows an ongoing "Audio boost is on" notification so these features keep working during calls and when you use other apps. The service stops when you turn the features off.
  • Wake lock and keyguard: used with the proximity sensor so the screen behaves correctly while the phone is held to your ear.
  • Proximity sensor: read on the device only, to switch the screen and audio routing during calls. Sensor data never leaves your device.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads (see below).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may automatically collect and share the following information to serve, measure, and personalise ads, for analytics, and to prevent fraud:

  • Device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App interactions, such as ad views and taps and app launches
  • Diagnostics, such as crash logs and performance data related to ads

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled under Google's own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

4. Your Choices and Consent

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the App asks for your consent (using Google's consent tool) before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the App.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Optional rewarded ads are shown only when you choose to watch one.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not store your data on our own servers. Settings stored on your device are deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Information collected by Google AdMob is kept and deleted according to Google's retention policies; you can manage or delete it through your Google Account and by resetting your advertising ID. If you have any request about your data, email us at goldappslabz@gmail.com.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

The App does not transmit any data to us. Communication between the App and Google's advertising services uses encrypted connections (HTTPS).

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at goldappslabz@gmail.com.
