This Privacy Policy describes how Gold Apps Labz ("we", "us", or "our") handles your information when you use the GPS Map Camera: Geotag Photo mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

Location Data: The App requires access to your device's GPS location to provide its core functionality: stamping location data onto your photos. This data is processed locally on your device for the purpose of geotagging.

Camera and Storage: We require access to your camera to take photos and storage to save the stamped images. These files remain on your device unless you choose to share them manually.

Device Information: We may collect non-personal information such as device model, OS version, and unique identifiers for advertising and analytics purposes through third-party SDKs (AdMob, Unity Ads).

2. Third-Party Services

The App uses third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google Play Services
  • AdMob
  • Unity Ads

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect personal information on our own servers, we do not retain your data. Any data generated (like photos) is stored on your device and is deleted if you delete the files or uninstall the App.

4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
